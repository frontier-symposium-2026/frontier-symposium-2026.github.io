About the symposium

The 2026 Frontier Symposium in Computational and Experimental Biophysics and Bioscience is designed to create sustained conversation between researchers working across methodological boundaries.

The meeting will combine invited lectures, contributed presentations, poster discussions, and informal networking. Program details are currently placeholders and will be updated by the organizing committee.

Who should attend?

Researchers at every career stage who work in biophysics, bioscience, structural biology, simulation, quantitative biology, imaging, spectroscopy, computation, or related areas.
