Program

Connecting models, experiments, and new ideas

The 2026 program brings together frontier research in computational science, chemistry, biophysics, and experimental biology. It emphasizes the integration of AI and other new computational methods with advanced experimental techniques.

Computational innovation

AI, models, and simulations that open new ways to investigate chemical and biological systems.

Experimental advances

Techniques that connect measurement and observation with mechanistic understanding.

Integrated biophysics

Research that brings computational and experimental approaches into closer dialogue.

Emerging methodologies

New tools, approaches, and combinations that can shape the next phase of the field.

Young scientist exchange

An international platform for early-career researchers to showcase their work and form new connections.

Regional collaboration

Academic exchange among China, Japan, South Korea, and the wider international research community.
